# Resource: yet_settings_sso_app

A tool of the company's own — its CRM, its intranet — that signs its people in with Yet: an OpenID Connect client, its issuer `https://auth.<base domain>`, or a SAML service provider, Yet's IdP metadata at `https://auth.<base domain>/saml/v2/metadata`. What it is told says the person's `groups` (the addresses of the company's lists they are on), their `role`, `admin` or `member`, and the company's `workspace`.

-> **Note:** Its `client_secret` is shown only as it is made, and kept in the state; an imported tool has none there.

!> **Warning:** Destroying it, or changing its kind or Entity ID, stops its "Sign in with Yet" — a new kind or Entity ID is a new client.

## Example Usage

```terraform
# The company's CRM signs its sales team in with Yet, and has a tile in
# their apps menu. Its client goes to the CRM's configuration.
resource "yet_settings_sso_app" "crm" {
  name          = "CRM"
  redirect_uris = ["https://crm.acme.com.br/auth/callback"]
  allowed = [
    yet_settings_person.ana.email,
    "vendas@acme.com.br",
  ]
  in_menu     = true
  home_url    = "https://crm.acme.com.br"
  privacy_url = "https://crm.acme.com.br/privacy"
  terms_url   = "https://crm.acme.com.br/terms"
}

output "crm_client_id" {
  value = yet_settings_sso_app.crm.client_id
}

output "crm_client_secret" {
  value     = yet_settings_sso_app.crm.client_secret
  sensitive = true
}

# The company's wiki signs everyone in over SAML: its Entity ID, and its ACS
# URL. The wiki reads Yet's IdP from https://auth.yet.com.br/saml/v2/metadata.
resource "yet_settings_sso_app" "wiki" {
  name          = "Wiki"
  kind          = "saml"
  entity_id     = "https://wiki.acme.com.br/saml"
  redirect_uris = ["https://wiki.acme.com.br/saml/acs"]
  everyone      = true
}
```

## Schema

### Required

- `name` (String) What people see as they sign in: `CRM`.
- `redirect_uris` (List of String) Where it sends people back after they sign in: https, or http on localhost to try it.

### Optional

- `allowed` (Set of String) The people and groups (the company's lists) who may sign in, by address, lowercase — only the company's.
- `entity_id` (String) A `saml` tool's Entity ID, which it needs — `https://wiki.acme.com/saml`, up to 1000 characters, no spaces. Changing it makes the tool again.
- `everyone` (Boolean) Whether all of the company may sign in — today and whoever joins — or only those `allowed`.
- `home_url` (String) Where its tile goes: the tool's own address, https. When not given, the site it sends people back to.
- `icon` (String) Its tile's image, a data: URL of a PNG, JPEG, WebP or SVG under 75 KB: `"data:image/png;base64,${filebase64("crm.png")}"`. Its initial when not given.
- `in_menu` (Boolean) Whether it has a tile in the apps menu of those who may sign in, from the moment they may. On unless turned off.
- `kind` (String) `server`, a tool that keeps a secret, `browser`, one in a browser or a phone, with none (PKCE), or `saml`, a SAML service provider, with an `entity_id`. Changing it makes the tool again.
- `privacy_url` (String) Its own privacy policy, https, linked where people sign in to it.
- `terms_url` (String) Its own terms, https, linked where people sign in to it.

### Read-Only

- `client_id` (String) Its OpenID Connect client id; a SAML tool's is its `entity_id`.
- `client_secret` (String, Sensitive) Its client secret, for a `server` tool; null for a `browser` or `saml` one, and for one imported.
- `id` (String) The tool's id.
