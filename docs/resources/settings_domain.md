# Resource: yet_settings_domain

One of the company's domains: whoever has an address at it may sign in, and with `mail`, its mail is here — mailboxes, lists, sending.

-> **Note:** A domain becomes the company's once its DNS shows it: the TXT record of its `yet_settings_domain_claim` at its root, or its zone in the company's Cloud answering. Creating this resource checks every 15 seconds for up to 10 minutes; a domain the company has already is taken hold of.

~> **Note:** Mail, once on, stays on: `mail` cannot go back to `false`. Without mail, destroying the domain leaves it the company's — only Yet's operator lets such a domain go.

!> **Warning:** With mail, destroying the domain takes it off the mail server and out of the company. Yet refuses while anyone has a mailbox at it.

## Example Usage

```terraform
# The company's once its TXT answers; then its mail, here.
resource "yet_settings_domain" "br" {
  name = yet_settings_domain_claim.br.name
  mail = true

  depends_on = [yet_cloud_dns_record.proof]
}
```

## Schema

### Required

- `name` (String) The domain, lowercase: `acme.dev`.

### Optional

- `mail` (Boolean) Whether its mail is here: the domain made on the mail server and in the company's SES, a mailbox for each of its people already in. Its MX must point here for mail to arrive.

### Read-Only

- `id` (String) The domain.
- `verified_by` (String) What showed the domain the company's: `txt` or `cloud` — empty when it was the company's already.
